I am a Ph.D. student in the joint Biostatistics program at Southern Methodist University and UT Southwestern Medical Center.

My research centers on time-dependent covariate survival analysis, missing data imputation and predictive modeling, and the development of AI agents for biomedical research and clinical trials. I am advised by Dr. Wenqi Shi.

I previously worked as a biostatistician at the Cleveland Clinic and as a data science intern at NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital. In these roles, I provided statistical consulting across a broad range of clinical areas, including anesthesiology, cardiology, pediatrics, and oncology. I collaborate closely with clinicians to design, analyze, and interpret clinical research studies.
